WHO WE ARE

Empowering People Through Practical HR Training

Shivaaksha HR Training Academy focuses on practical, career-oriented human resource training. We help learners understand the core responsibilities of HR professionals and develop the skills required in today's workplace.

Our learning approach covers recruitment and selection, onboarding, employee engagement, payroll fundamentals, HR operations, labour-law awareness and professional communication through practical examples and workplace-based activities.

We aim to build confidence, workplace readiness and a strong foundation for individuals who want to begin or grow their careers in Human Resources.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible and practical HR education that develops job-ready skills. We focus on real workplace processes, professional ethics, communication and people-management practices so learners can contribute confidently to organisations.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted learning partner for aspiring HR professionals by encouraging continuous development, practical knowledge and responsible people management. We strive to help learners build meaningful careers and support better workplaces.
